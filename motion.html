Motion

Motion work across two contexts: directing and producing audiovisual material at Incode to show FanID's verification experience in real-world use — not just the interface, but the moment itself — and, earlier at CreatixBox, animating full product interfaces from the ground up in After Effects.

Incode — directing real-world verification

I directed, animated, and post-produced commercial and client-facing material showing FanID's onboarding and verification flow embedded directly into phone screens — while a production director worked alongside me to guide the actors, consulting me on how the onboarding actually worked. These pieces showed identity verification across different real-life contexts: onboarding into a stadium match, verifying identity in a rideshare, checking into a hotel room. Used both for client meetings — to show simplified onboarding and UX to prospects — and for commercial material at industry conventions in Las Vegas and New York. I also built a modular video design system so this content could be adapted per client without rebuilding each piece from scratch.
